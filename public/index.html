The Narrative

An Evening Above the Ordinary.

SOHO emerges as Ludhiana’s most coveted nocturnal retreat—an atmospheric sanctuary suspended between city lights and celestial skies. Conceived for epicures, tastemakers, and discerning travelers, our space fuses open-air architectural freedom with warm candlelight intimacy.

Here, progressive global cuisines converse with artisanal botanical mixology. Whether observing twilight soften across Sarabha Nagar or immersed in midnight low-tempo lounge sets, every element is composed to linger in memory.
